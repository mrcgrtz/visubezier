VisuBezier 2.1.0
================

A new "show_track" setting, and noticeably faster previews.

show_track
----------

The preview normally pairs the plotted curve with an animation track: two
blocks travelling alongside it, yours against the reference easing. Set

    "show_track": false

for a preview of nothing but the curve. Nothing moves in that mode, so
"animate" has nothing left to do, and the reference easing is neither drawn
nor labelled.

Settings live under:
    Preferences > Package Settings > VisuBezier > Settings

Faster previews
---------------

Rendering is roughly 2.9 times faster. Filling rectangles, which took most
of the time spent on every frame, no longer works pixel by pixel. The
pictures themselves are unchanged.
